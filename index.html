Food + café

Fixed brunch

Bistro Rojiura ~¥2k–3k pp

Small back-alley bistro with a cult brunch dish. The appeal is the contrast between custardy toast, crisp caramelized edges, salty house bacon and creamy burrata.

Recommended: French toast with homemade bacon & burrata (listed around ¥1,590). Share the scrambled egg, avocado & homemade ham open sandwich if you want something savory too.
Backup brunch: if the wait is unreasonable, keep brunch simple nearby rather than sacrificing the museum slot.
Café · Marunouchi

Saza Coffee · KITTE

Convenient after the museum and more memorable than a generic chain stop. It is known for its strong, dark-roasted Shogun Coffee.

Recommended: Shogun Coffee; if GF prefers milk drinks, Shogun café latte.
Backup café: ROKUMEI COFFEE Gransta Marunouchi.
Fixed dinner

Unagi no Naruse · Caretta Shiodome from ~¥1,600

Accessible unagi pricing, easy route from Marunouchi, and the Caretta branch is open for dinner until 20:00.

Recommended: middle-size unaju if available, around the ¥2,500 tier. It gives you a proper portion while staying well under your ¥5,000 cap.
Backup: Caretta Shiodome has several casual Japanese restaurants if there is an unexpected closure or long queue.
Budget note

Saturday stays comfortably under cap

Rojiura brunch + unagi dinner gives you two destination meals without paying omakase or luxury-yakiniku prices.

Target: roughly ¥4,000–5,000 per person across both main meals before drinks.

Food + cafés

Akihabara lunch

Flexible casual lunch ~¥1k–2.5k

Do not queue an hour for a destination lunch on the Steins;Gate day. The point is to leave enough time for both Akihabara and Shibuya.

Recommended: tonkatsu, curry or gyukatsu near the route depending on queue. Tonkatsu Marugo is good only if the wait is sensible.
Backup: eat inside/around Yodobashi if everything nearby is packed.
Akihabara café

KIELO COFFEE

Specialty coffee without turning the break into another attraction.

Recommended: pour-over if you drink black; latte/flat white if not.
Backup café: Sarutahiko Coffee at atre Akihabara.
Shibuya café

Blue Bottle Shibuya

Useful location near PARCO/Udagawacho and a good place to physically stop after a few hours on your feet.

Recommended: iced coffee or latte; keep it simple before dinner.
Backup café: The Roastery by Nozy Coffee near Cat Street.
Fixed dinner

Maa-san’s House ¥2k–3k typical

Known for an extremely soft 100% wagyu hamburger eaten with a spoon. This is more distinctive than another generic yakiniku meal.

Recommended: 200 g wagyu “toro-toro” hamburger + rice, around ¥2,480; the larger set with soup/salad/rice is still under budget.
Backup: choose a casual Shibuya hamburger/teppan restaurant under ¥3,000 rather than replacing it with an expensive wagyu course.

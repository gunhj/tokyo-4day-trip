Food + cafés

Fixed lunch

Maa-san’s House ~¥2k–3k

Casual meat specialist and distinctive enough to deserve a fixed slot.

Recommended: 200 g wagyu “toro-toro” hamburger + rice.
Backup: another Shibuya hamburger/teppan lunch under ¥3,000 if the wait is excessive.
Shopping café

The Roastery by Nozy Coffee

Convenient around Cat Street and better suited to this walking route than detouring elsewhere.

Recommended: single-origin espresso or café latte.
Backup: Blue Bottle Shibuya earlier in the day.
Dinner / drinks

Casual Shinjuku izakaya ~¥2k–4k

Prioritize atmosphere, grilled food and good highballs rather than a formal dinner.

Recommended: yakitori, karaage, potato salad, grilled skewers + kaku/whisky highballs.
Backup: pick any busy, well-reviewed izakaya around Shinjuku-sanchome if the first choice is full.
Optional extra

Bistro Rojiura French toast

Keep this only as a bonus if you unexpectedly have a free Saturday brunch window. Do not distort the itinerary for it.

Recommended: French toast with homemade bacon + burrata.
Skip it this trip if timing is awkward.

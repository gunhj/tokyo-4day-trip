Coffee stop

NEZUCAFÉ

Best coffee stop for the architecture day because it is part of the museum and looks into the garden, so the break is still part of the spatial experience.

Order: coffee + seasonal cake, or just a hot black coffee if you want to keep moving.
Backup: KOFFEE MAMEYA in Aoyama for a more serious hand-brew experience.
Dinner

KUROOBI Roppongi

Convenient after the architecture walk and better aligned with the “good pork ramen in Tokyo” craving than a lighter yuzu/shio bowl.

Order: rich pork-broth ramen with chashu + egg; add extra noodles if still hungry.
Backup: Ippudo Roppongi. Less unique, but very reliable Hakata-style tonkotsu and easy late hours.

Lunch

Manten Sushi Marunouchi

Good-value omakase compared with ultra-high-end Tokyo sushi, and it fits perfectly with the museum location.

Order: standard omakase course rather than the pricier tuna-focused upgrade.
Backup: Nemuro Hanamaru at KITTE for a much more casual, lively sushi meal.
Cafe

Saza Coffee · KITTE

A genuinely Japanese coffee stop with a historical angle rather than just a convenient chain.

Order: Shogun Coffee hot; Shogun Café Latte if GF prefers milk coffee.
Backup: Rokumei Coffee Gransta Marunouchi for convenience inside the station complex.
Dinner

Tsunahachi · Tokyo Daimaru

Traditional tempura in an easy Tokyo Station location, so dinner does not become another cross-city mission.

Order: tempura set with shrimp, fish and seasonal vegetables.
Backup: casual soba/udon around KITTE if you are still full from sushi.

Lunch

Tonkatsu Marugo

Specialist tonkatsu and a better Tokyo-specific lunch than eating inside Yodobashi.

Order: rosu-katsu for richer flavour; hire-katsu if you prefer very tender and lean.
Backup: Gyukatsu Ichinisan. Order the standard gyukatsu set and cook slices on the hot stone.
Akihabara cafe

KIELO COFFEE

Specialty coffee without making the break into another tourist attraction.

Order: pour-over if you drink black; flat white/latte if not.
Backup: Sarutahiko Coffee at atre Akihabara for maximum convenience.
Shibuya cafe

Blue Bottle Shibuya

Convenient to PARCO and Udagawacho, and the Kitaya Park setting makes it a useful actual rest stop.

Order: New Orleans-style iced coffee if available, otherwise a latte.
Backup: The Roastery by Nozy Coffee near Cat Street. Try single-origin espresso or latte.
Dinner

Wagyu yakiniku in Shibuya

After a long shopping day, a grill-at-the-table dinner is fun and doesn't require a delicate tasting-menu mood.

Order: mixed wagyu platter with both lean and marbled cuts; add rice/kimchi as needed.
Backup: another well-rated yakiniku near your final shopping stop rather than crossing Shibuya again.
